Privacy Policy

ThreadMark Workwear ("ThreadMark," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, company, email address and phone number when you request a quote, subscribe to our newsletter, or create an account. We also collect uploaded logo files for embroidery digitization purposes.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to quote requests and process orders
  • To send order and account-related communications
  • To send newsletter content, if you've opted in
  • To improve our products and website experience

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business (such as payment processors, shipping carriers and email delivery services), under confidentiality obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting privacy@threadmarkworkwear.com.
