How It Works

From First Call to Delivered Uniforms

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share your team size, industry and timeline — no account required to get started.

  2. Choose uniforms

    Pick styles from our catalogue or send us a reference garment to match.

  3. Send your logo

    Upload artwork in PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG or vector format for digitization.

  4. Approve embroidery proof

    Review placement, size and thread colors before we cut a single stitch.

  5. Confirm quantities

    Finalize sizes and quantities across your team roster.

  6. Production

    Garments are cut, embroidered and quality-checked in our facility.

  7. Delivery

    Orders ship complete, with reorder profiles saved for next time.
