Turn Your Logo Into Professional Embroidery.

Upload your artwork, choose a garment and placement, and review a free digital proof before we stitch a single unit.

Embroidery workflow steps

  1. Upload your logo

    PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, or vector AI/EPS files accepted.

  2. Select garment

    Choose from our catalogue or tell us your own garment.

  3. Choose placement

    Left chest, right chest, sleeve, back or cap.

  4. Choose thread colors

    Matched to your brand palette during digitization.

  5. Review digital proof

    We send a mockup before any stitching begins.

  6. Approve production

    Sign off and we schedule your run.

  7. Embroidery & quality check

    Every unit is inspected before it ships.
