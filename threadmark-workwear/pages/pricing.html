Uniform & Embroidery Pricing

Pricing on uniforms depends on garment, fabric, quantity and embroidery specifications — here's exactly what drives the number.

What Determines Your Price

Pricing Factors

Garment Type

Polos, shirts and fleece cost less than structured jackets and technical outerwear.

Fabric

Performance and technical fabrics carry a premium over standard cotton-poly blends.

Quantity

Per-unit price drops at 50, 100, 250 and 500+ unit thresholds.

Embroidery Size

Larger embroidery areas require more stitches and more production time.

Locations & Colors

Each additional placement or thread color beyond the first adds a small per-unit cost.

Digitization

A one-time stitch-file setup fee, waived on reorders using an existing logo file.

Special Finishing

Anti-microbial, flame-resistant or waterproof treatments carry an added cost.

Delivery

Standard ground shipping is included; rush and multi-location delivery is quoted separately.
