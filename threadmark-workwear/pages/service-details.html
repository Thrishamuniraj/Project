Executive Corporate Polo Shirt

A tailored-fit polo built for client-facing teams that need to look sharp through a full workday — reinforced collar, side vents and a clean chest area for embroidery.

  • Minimum order: 12 units per color/size combination
  • Production time: 7–10 business days after proof approval
  • Suitable for: Corporate teams, retail staff, hospitality front-of-house

Product information

Fabric

65% cotton / 35% polyester piqué knit, 210 GSM. Pre-shrunk and garment-washed for a consistent fit after laundering.

Fit

Tailored fit through the body with a slightly dropped shoulder seam. Relaxed fit available on request for bulk orders.

Construction

Reinforced three-button placket, knit collar and cuffs, side vents for movement, and a clean left-chest panel sized for embroidery up to 4" wide.

Customization Options

Left chest, right chest, sleeve and back embroidery are all available on this style. Thread color is matched to your brand palette during the proofing step.

  • ✓ Left chest logo (most common)
  • ✓ Sleeve embroidery
  • ✓ Back embroidery for larger emblems

Care Instructions

  • Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors
  • Tumble dry low or hang to dry to protect embroidery
  • Do not iron directly over embroidered areas
  • Do not dry clean with solvent-based chemicals
