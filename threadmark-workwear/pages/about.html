Built by People Who Take Uniforms Seriously.

ThreadMark has supplied embroidered uniforms and workwear to businesses across the country since 2011 — this is how we got here.

Our Story

From a Single Embroidery Machine to a Full Production Facility

ThreadMark started in 2011 with one embroidery machine and a handful of restaurant clients who needed consistent, professional-looking staff uniforms. Word spread through referrals, and by 2016 we had outgrown our first facility.

Today we operate a dedicated cutting, embroidery and quality-control floor, supplying uniform programs for hospitality groups, hospital networks, construction firms and corporate offices across the country — while still personally reviewing every embroidery proof before it goes to production.

Our Mission

Make it simple for any business to outfit its entire team in consistent, professional, properly branded uniforms — regardless of size or industry.

Our Vision

Be the uniform partner businesses stay with for a decade — not because switching is hard, but because reordering is effortless.

Quality Standards

Every Unit Is Inspected Before It Ships

Consistency is the entire point of a uniform program — one mismatched shirt undermines the whole team's look. That's why every order passes through a manual quality check before packing.
