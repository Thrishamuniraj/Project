Branding Methods

Seven Ways to Apply Your Logo

We match the method to your product, budget and artwork — every option below is available in-house.

Screen Printing

Bold, vibrant color on apparel and flat surfaces. Ideal for simple logos at scale.

Embroidery

A stitched, textured finish for polos, caps and jackets — durable and premium.

Digital Printing

Full-color, photo-realistic logos with no minimum color count.

UV Printing

Sharp, durable prints on curved and rigid surfaces like bottles and tech.

Laser Engraving

A permanent, single-tone etch into metal, wood or bamboo surfaces.

Heat Transfer

Full-color designs applied by heat press — great for small-batch apparel runs.

Custom Packaging

Branded boxes, sleeves and inserts that turn unboxing into a brand moment.

Not Sure Which to Pick?

Tell us your product and logo — we'll recommend the best method for the result and budget.
