Privacy Policy

BrandCraft Merchandise ("we", "us") collects the information you submit through our quote, bulk order and custom branding forms — including your name, company, email, phone number and project details — solely to prepare quotations and fulfil orders.

Information We Collect

Contact details you provide via forms; uploaded logo files; and standard web analytics (pages visited, browser type) collected through cookies as described in our Cookie Policy below.

How We Use Your Information

To respond to quote requests, process orders, provide customer support, and — only if you opt in — send occasional product and industry updates. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain quote and order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order and comply with accounting requirements, typically no longer than seven years.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@brandcraftmerchandise.com.
