We're a quotation-based supplier, not a flat-rate catalogue. Every price is shaped by the product itself, how many you order, and how it's branded — so you only pay for what your project actually needs.

What Shapes Your Quote

Ten Factors That Determine Price

Product Type

A pen and a power bank start from very different cost bases.

Material

Stainless steel, bamboo and recycled fabrics all price differently.

Quantity

Unit cost drops as order quantity increases.

Branding Method

Embroidery, UV and laser engraving carry different setup costs.

Branding Locations

Each additional logo placement adds setup and run time.

Number of Colors

More ink colors mean more screens or passes in production.

Artwork Preparation

Vector-ready logos are faster and cheaper to prepare than raster art.

Packaging

Custom boxes, sleeves and inserts add material and labor cost.

Delivery

Single-address vs. multi-location shipping changes logistics cost.

Production Timeline

Rush production carries a premium over standard lead times.
