About BrandCraft Merchandise

We Help Businesses Turn Products Into Brand Experiences.

Founded in 2011, BrandCraft Merchandise has grown from a two-person print shop into a full-service promotional products partner for 500+ companies — without losing the hands-on quality control that got us started.

Our Story

Started With One Screen Printer and a Simple Idea

BrandCraft Merchandise began in a small studio with a single screen-printing press and a conviction that promotional products deserved better quality control than the industry standard. Fifteen years later, that conviction still shapes how we run every order — every proof is checked by a person, not just a machine.

Today we source, brand and ship merchandise for corporate offices, startups, conferences, schools and nonprofits across the country, but the studio mindset never left: small enough to catch mistakes before they reach you, established enough to handle a 5,000-unit rollout without missing a delivery date.

Our Mission

To make it simple for any business to put a polished, on-brand product in front of the people who matter to them — employees, customers, and everyone in between.

Our Vision

A promotional products industry known for quality and reliability first — where "branded merchandise" means something a recipient actually wants to keep and use.

How We Work

Sourcing, Branding and Quality Control — In-House

Product Sourcing

Direct relationships with vetted manufacturers across apparel, drinkware, tech and gifting categories.

Branding Expertise

An in-house art and production team fluent in seven branding methods.

Quality Control

Every production run is spot-checked against the approved proof before it ships.

Production Capability

In-house screen printing, embroidery and UV printing, with trusted partners for specialty finishes.

Customer Support

A dedicated account manager on every order, from first quote to reorder.

15 Years of Experience

Thousands of production runs across every product category we carry.
